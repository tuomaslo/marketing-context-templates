## Claims and examples

- Use [Product and proof](../proof/product-and-proof.md) for factual claims.
- Keep Proposed wording separate from Confirmed facts. Leave out Unknown claims.
- Use exact sourced quotes; do not present suggested customer language as a quote.
- Do not promise results, availability, or superiority without supporting evidence.
- Label new examples Draft. Published does not mean high-performing.

## Before publishing

Every answer should be **yes**:

- Does this sound like our brand rather than interchangeable copy?
- Is it useful and understandable to the intended audience?
- Are factual claims supported and current for the audience and plan?
- Are examples and quotes represented accurately?
- Does it follow the channel and brand rules?
- Is the next action clear, with working destinations where needed?

If an answer is no, revise that part before publishing.
