# Brand Core — Linear

## What we do

**Confirmed:** Linear builds software for coordinating product development by teams and AI agents. Its public company description emphasizes a shared, structured working environment. [Source: About](https://linear.app/about), checked 5 October 2026.

See [Product and proof](../proof/product-and-proof.md) for capabilities and plans.

## Who we serve

**Proposed:** People responsible for planning and building software products, especially teams that need clear ownership and coordination across product, design, and engineering.

See [Audience](audience-profile.md) for the working hypotheses.

## Mission and direction

**Proposed mission:** Help product teams turn direction into coordinated work and useful software.

**Confirmed direction:** Linear describes its company as building for the next era of product development, including work shared by people and agents. [Source: About](https://linear.app/about).

## Principles

**Confirmed public principles, paraphrased:** Design tools around creators, reduce administrative work, and make priorities understandable. [Source: Linear Method](https://linear.app/method/introduction).

## One-line pitch

**Proposed:** Linear gives product teams a shared place to plan work, coordinate delivery, and track progress.

## Brand promise

**Proposed:** Your team can understand what matters and keep the work moving.
