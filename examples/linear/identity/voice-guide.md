# Voice Guide — Linear

## Personality

**Proposed:** Precise, composed, and focused on the work. These are writing choices inferred from public examples, for this educational pack.

## Writing rules

**Proposed:**

- Lead with the task or the change for the user.
- Use product nouns consistently: issues, projects, and cycles.
- Explain what someone can do before adding a broad benefit.
- Keep headings short and explanations specific.
- For a release, say what changed and how to use it.

The emphasis on understandable terms is supported by [Linear Method](https://linear.app/method/introduction), checked 5 October 2026.

## Examples

**Published:** “The system for modern product development”. [Source: Features](https://linear.app/features), checked 5 October 2026. Performance: **Unknown**. A direct category statement makes a useful reference.

**Draft:** “Keep the launch work together. Group related issues in a project so your team can follow the same outcome.”

**Revision:**

- Before: “Unlock unparalleled alignment with a revolutionary collaboration experience.”
- After: “Group related issues in a project and track progress together.”
- Why: The revised draft describes an actual action.

## Words we use

**Proposed:** Name the specific object or action. Replace generic productivity claims with a concrete workflow. See [Guardrails](../guardrails/guardrails.md).
