# Audience Profile — Linear

## Primary audience

**Proposed:** Product managers, engineers, and designers building software together. Team leads need the shared view; individual contributors need clarity about their own work.

This interpretation draws on [Linear Method](https://linear.app/method/introduction) and [project documentation](https://linear.app/docs/projects), checked 5 October 2026.

## Their job and goals

**Proposed:**

- **Job:** Turn a product plan into owned, actionable work.
- **Trigger:** A launch or initiative requires coordination across people or teams.
- **Result:** Everyone can see the outcome, owner, and current progress.

## Friction and objections

**Proposed:** Unclear priorities, scattered project context, and uncertainty about who owns delivery. A potential adoption concern is changing an established workflow.

## What they know

**Proposed:** Assume familiarity with product development. Explain how a particular Linear object or workflow helps the task at hand.
