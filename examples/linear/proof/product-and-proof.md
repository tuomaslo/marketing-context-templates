# Product and Proof — Linear

## Product

**Confirmed:** Projects group related issues around an outcome or target completion date. A project can span multiple teams and include documents and milestones. [Source: Projects](https://linear.app/docs/projects), checked 5 October 2026.

## Capabilities and limits

**Confirmed:** Issues, projects, cycles, and initiatives are listed as core capabilities. Feature eligibility differs by plan. [Source: Pricing](https://linear.app/pricing), checked 5 October 2026.

**Confirmed:** Coding sessions and Loops use a separate workspace credit balance on eligible paid plans. A subscription price alone does not describe every AI usage charge. [Source: AI credits](https://linear.app/docs/ai-credits), checked 5 October 2026.

## Plans

**Confirmed public pricing snapshot:**

| Plan | Price and billing basis | Selected limits |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Unlimited members; 2 teams; 250 issues |
| Basic | $10 per user/month, billed yearly | 5 teams; unlimited issues |
| Business | $16 per user/month, billed yearly | Unlimited teams; additional features |
| Enterprise | Custom, annual billing | Contact sales for terms |

[Source: Pricing](https://linear.app/pricing), checked **5 October 2026**. Recheck before using prices or eligibility in new copy.
